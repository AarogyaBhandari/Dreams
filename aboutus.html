About DREAMS Foundation

DREAMS Foundation works with responders, volunteers, government and non-government organizations to strengthen disaster preparedness, response, and recovery through research, consultation, training, and innovative disaster risk management approaches at national and regional levels.

Established in Nepal, DREAMS Foundation is dedicated to building resilient communities. Our team brings experience in emergency management, logistics, and capacity building.

Preparedness creates stronger communities

We work alongside partners and communities to support practical, proactive approaches to disaster risk reduction.

Our history

Founded in 2022, DREAMS Foundation emerged from the need for specialized disaster management services in Nepal. In response to climate-related disasters and urban challenges, we focus on proactive measures and community empowerment. Since our inception, we have partnered with local governments, international organizations, and communities to implement disaster risk reduction strategies across Nepal and beyond.
