Mission, Vision & Motto

Mission

To enhance the capacity of responders, volunteers, private institutions, government and non-government agencies in disaster risk reduction and management through consultation services, research, training, awareness programs and logistics support.

Vision

To be a centre of excellence in disaster risk reduction and management in Nepal.

Motto

Safety is for life.
Be alert, Be prepared.

What guides our work

Organizational Objectives

Practical action and collaboration to help people prepare for, respond to and recover from disasters.

  1. 01

    Policy & expert advice

    Provide consultation, advisory, research and expert support for disaster risk reduction policy and planning.

  2. 02

    Emergency resources

    Acquire and supply emergency and rescue equipment, plus relief items for preparedness, response and recovery.

  3. 03

    Training & awareness

    Conduct training, public awareness and capacity-building programmes in disaster risk reduction and management.

  4. 04

    Partnerships & coordination

    Work with national and international agencies to strengthen and promote disaster risk reduction.

  5. 05

    Skilled technical teams

    Develop skilled personnel to maintain and preserve disaster management equipment and laboratories.

  6. 06

    DRR collaboration

    Participate in disaster risk reduction networks, meetings, training and joint programmes.

  7. 07

    Rapid response readiness

    Mobilize skilled disaster response teams with rescue and relief equipment in Nepal and abroad.

Key Services

Consultation & Policy Support

Technical advisory and strategic planning for disaster risk reduction and resilience.

Training & Capacity Building

Workshops, awareness programs, simulation exercises and skill-based training.

Logistics & Emergency Support

Supply of rescue tools, safety equipment and disaster response support services.
